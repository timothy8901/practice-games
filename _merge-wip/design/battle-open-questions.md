# Battle core: open rules questions for the design lead

Raised by the engineers who built and verified `proto/battle/` (212 tests green). Each needs a one-line decision in GDD-00-decisions.md or the tuning table.

## Open questions (builder)
- Proposed rules I implemented one way and the design lead should confirm: Scrap's 1/8 HP cost applies even when a Ward blocks it (rules 9.2 Proposed); Capture Stance's 1-HP floor also stops poison/burn/trap chip on the wild creature (doc says 'party damage'; I floored every non-self source so a capture target can never be KO'd by accident); a second Vesper Mend while one is pending fails without spending a Mend (doc is silent).
- Crackdust duration: implemented as 3 full attacking turns after the turn it is thrown (the throw itself uses the turn). If the lead reads '3 turns' as including the throw turn, change CRACKDUST_TURNS + 1 to CRACKDUST_TURNS in engine._useItem.
- Shoresense auto-Reads for free even on Freefall (where a manual Read costs the turn). This seemed right for an ability, but it makes a Sloshkin lead a strong Freefall pick; confirm.
- Freefall Shift odds remain [NEED SOURCE] in the Sloshkin table (null), as in the doc; the engine does not need them but the Forecast system will.
- Doubles (rules section 11) are not in the engine, matching ARCHITECTURE-DRAFT 5.1's '[GDD: doubles yes/no]'; Skein Sweep's spread flag only pops all decoys today.
- makeCreature's no-rng/no-uid fallback is a module-level counter (deterministic per creation order, but not per seed). The architecture plans string uids from systems/party.nextUid; pass opts.uid once that exists.
- Base stats, catch rates, EXP yields, growth curves, the 0.85-1.0 damage roll, status chip fractions, the capture formula constants and the 40 x level default payout are all invented tuning values; the pitch gives none.

## Spec gaps noted by the verifier
- Ward badge state lied to the UI: the "Ward ?" badge was tracked per side, so after a Ward creature switched out the non-Ward replacement still showed the badge (rules 8.2 ties the badge to the Ward creature). Fixed: badge is now per creature uid (follows switches; on Freefall it is remembered once a hit was blocked).
- Keen Glint (creatures 3.9, Proposed): the start-of-turn glint only used the ability's +2 stages, so a Reap (+1 high-crit) could crit without the ears glinting. Fixed: the glint uses the chosen technique's full crit stage, so glint <=> crit (tested over 200 battles).
- Ward readout (rules 8.3 item 1 + 3): a party with no Strong technique but a status chip move (e.g. Spore Drift vs Prism Ward) read "Nothing in your party lands right now" with only a generic indirect list; the chip technique was never named by creature and move. Fixed: readout now carries `chip: [{creature, move, kind, pp}]` (status chip moves the Ward lets through, or damaging chip moves that land; a status already on the target drops that move, traps stay) and the text names them; the simulator checks this invariant at emission time.
- Architecture 5.1 action shape: the draft names the item action `{type:'item', id}` but the engine only accepted `{item}`; `id` was silently treated as 'No item undefined'. Fixed: both keys accepted (engine + ai.isLegal).
- Crackdust (rules 8.4) could be submitted against a creature with no Ward or an already-suppressed Ward and was silently consumed, although the menu said it was unselectable. Fixed: the engine refuses with BattleError 'NO_WARD' and the item is kept.
- `step(action)` threw 'Side 1 must switch in a replacement' whenever the UI passed its usual player action during a foe-only replacement phase (it fed the player's action into the foe slot). Fixed: the player action is ignored there and foeAi picks the replacement.
- state().boostBlocked (Plumbline, rules 10.2) was off by one for the UI: after the second blocked turn it still read true although the coming turn may boost; the trainer AI had the same off-by-one and skipped an evasion turn. Fixed: the snapshot describes the coming turn and adds `boostBlockedTurns`; the AI check corrected.
- Rules 15.4 / 17.1 Freefall shows bands only, but the 'capture-shake' and 'capture-fail' events still carried exact odds. Fixed: odds are null on a bands dial and a `nextBand` is added.
- Architecture 5.1 names `rules: DialRules`, `availableActions` move entries `{i, usable}`, `setup.nextUid` for a caught creature and (5.5) `holdOut`; none existed. Added: exported DIAL_RULES per dial (readCostsTurn, captureDisplay, wardBadge, runAllowed, holdOutTurns) overridable via setup.rules (all former hard-coded dial checks now read the rules), `i`/`usable` aliases on moves/switches/scrap/run/read/lantern/items, `nextUid` applied on capture, and `holdOut: N` ending the battle as outcome 'holdout' (no blackout, no EXP from standing foes) for the opening scene.
- read(side) after the battle ended was not refused (submit was). Fixed: BattleError 'ENDED'.
- Dead code: freshVol().hollowHitThisTurn was set and never read. Removed.
- Not fixed / by design (documented for the lead): Doubles (rules 11) absent per ARCHITECTURE-DRAFT 5.1; Freefall Shift odds remain null [NEED SOURCE]; Crackdust = 3 full attacking turns after the throw turn; Capture Stance floor also applies to poison/burn/trap chip on the wild creature (doc says 'party damage'); Scrap's 1/8 cost applies when a Ward blocks it (9.2 Proposed); Mirrorveil (Proposed) also blocks Plumbline since it is a status technique, which would make a Mirrorveil + evasion creature unanswerable by the 7th-Spire disc; base stats, catch rates, EXP yields, growth curves, status chip fractions, the 0.85-1.0 roll, capture constants and the 40 x level payout are invented tuning values; makeCreature's no-uid fallback is a module counter until systems/party.nextUid exists.
