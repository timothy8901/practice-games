BLOB KNIGHT RUMPLER  (Android, version {version})
=================================================

WHAT'S IN HERE
  BlobKnightRumpler.apk   the game as an Android app. Plays offline.
  web/                    the same game as a web page (index.html + models/),
                          for putting on a website; see the bottom of this file

INSTALL ON YOUR PHONE  (Android 7.0 or newer)
  1. Get BlobKnightRumpler.apk onto the phone: download this zip on the phone and
     open it in the Files app, or copy the APK over by USB, Drive or email.
  2. Tap BlobKnightRumpler.apk.
  3. The first time, Android asks to allow installs from that app (Files, Chrome,
     Drive...). Tap Settings, turn on "Allow from this source", then go back.
  4. Tap Install, then Open.

  If Play Protect warns that it doesn't recognise the developer, tap "More details"
  then "Install anyway". The app isn't from the Play Store; it asks for no
  permissions apart from vibration.

HOW TO PLAY
  Move       drag your left thumb anywhere on the left half of the screen
  ATTACK     big pink button (bottom right)
  SPECIAL    purple button; its ring fills while it recharges and glows gold when ready
  SHIELD     blue button, hold it; the ring shows how much shield is left
  JUMP       green button; hop over swings and projectiles
  Pause      button at the top of the screen, or your phone's Back button
  Sound      button at the top of the screen, or on the menu

  Pick one of 8 knights, then knock the CPU's hearts out before it gets yours.
  Each knight is a core: Blade, Arc, Disc, Maul, Bow, Flare, Cinder or Veil,
  with its own attack and special.

  TWO MODES
    1v1        pick your knight and the challenger's (or leave it random), then
               set the rules: 1, 2, 3 or 5 KOs to win, a clock (or none) and how
               good the CPU is - Easy, Medium, Hard or Expert. A tied clock goes
               to sudden death.
    SURVIVAL   challenger after challenger, a tier harder every two rounds. Your
               hearts carry from round to round and nothing heals them, so the run
               ends when you go down. The menu remembers your best.

UPDATING
  A newer APK installs over this one (same signing key), no uninstall needed.

PLAY IT IN A BROWSER INSTEAD
  web/ holds the whole game: index.html plus its models. Put the folder on any
  website (your games page, itch.io as an HTML game) and open that link on a
  phone. It goes full screen on the first tap; hold the phone sideways. On a
  computer it plays with the keyboard: WASD or arrows to move, Space jump,
  H attack, J special, K shield.
