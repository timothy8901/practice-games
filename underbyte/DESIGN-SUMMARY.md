# UNDERBYTE — Design Summary (1-page digest of `DESIGN.md`)

`DESIGN.md` is canonical; this page is for quick reference only. Section numbers refer to it.

## Names

* **Fighters (5):** Tessik (The Courier Lantern, Signal, balanced) · Brackmaw (The Walking Vault, Ore, heavy) · Vellix (The Prefetch Streak, Flux, fast-fragile) · Orrubel (The Ring Warden, Cipher, zoner) · Korvask (The Clamp Balancer, Pressure, bruiser). Ids: `tessik brackmaw vellix orrubel korvask`.
* **Zones (5, in order):** Driftcache (`driftcache`, 1) → Loomworks (`loomworks`, 2) → Brinevault (`brinevault`, 3) → Nullspire (`nullspire`, 4) → Rootwell (`rootwell`, 5).
* **Bosses (4):** Tallock, Warden of the Stack (`dc_sentinel`, HP 120) → Weftyr, The Shuttle That Would Not Stop (`lw_weftyr`, HP 180) → Corrosant, Keeper of the Brine (`bv_corrosant`, HP 240) → Kernauth, The Root Process (`rw_throne`, phase 1 HP 200 seated, phase 2 HP 240 Unbound). **Boss ids (code, `room.boss.id`, `save.bossesDefeated`): `tallock weftyr corrosant kernauth`** — the room ids above are the arenas, not the boss ids (`UB.IDS.bossRooms`, CONTRACT §6.2).
* **Abilities (fixed order):** Echo Jump `echoJump` (boss 1) → Burst Line `burstLine` (boss 2) → Grip Protocol `gripProtocol` (boss 3).
* **Vendor:** Scrivvet (`dc_shrine` and `rw_rest`): Spare Cell 90 (max energy 125) · Hot Clock 160 (meter gain ×1.5) · Deep Ping 120 (map reveal); `rw_rest` sells at `deepPrice` 70 / 120 / 90 (`UB.Util.vendorPrice`, `game.buy` charges it).
* **Enemies (10 + 11 variants):** scuttlebit, hopnode, driftmoth (brinejelly), glarewisp (rootwisp), spitvalve (acid valve, twin valve), rambyte (rootram), platewalker (rootplate), broodcell (+motes), droplatch, voltwheel; scuttlebit variants brinecrab/static-shot/rootbound, hopnode dripnode/nullnode. Every variant is placed: the Rootwell ones (rootbound scuttlebit under the `rw_gate` pedestal, a rootwisp in its right half) joined the gauntlet in QA round 1.

Nodes: dc_wake (5,12) · dc_shrine (9,14) · lw_rest (15,14) · bv_rest (15,13) · ns_rest (15,14) · rw_rest (9,14). 25 reciprocal exit pairs; loops A (zones 1–2 via `lw_shaft`), B (2–3 via `bv_catwalk`), C (zone 4 via `ns_plunge.L2`). ## Key numbers

* 60 Hz fixed logic; canvas 960×540 logical; backing = dpr × CSS upscale quantised to 1/1.5/2 (crisp at 1080p on a dpr-1 display); every full-screen cache (parallax, fog, vignette) is built at that scale; tile 30 px. Fighters in px/s, enemies/bosses/movers in px/frame. Ambient layer per zone (§11.1.6) between bg2 and the tiles.
* Fighter stats (HP · run · jump · gravity · 2nd jump · dash · body): Tessik 5·235·640·1800·0.85·180/12f·22×48 — Brackmaw 6·180·620·2000·**1.00**·180/15f·34×54 — Vellix 4·300·**615**·1750·0.90·**210**/10f·18×40 — Orrubel 5·215·630·1800·0.85·180/12f·24×44 — Korvask 6·200·610·1900·**1.00**·180/14f·30×56.
* Jump apex 96–114 px (clears 3 tiles, fails 4); Echo-Jump apex 190–196 (clears 6, fails 7); dash 180 (Vellix 210), `vy=0`, 18 f cooldown, no i-frames; wall jump `vx=(run+40) away, vy=−0.95·jump`; chute launch 1.2·jump (never cut by a key release); 6 px ledge step-up; coyote 6 f (wall-coyote 5 f after the let-go frame); buffer 6 f; jump cut ×0.45; air-attack landing lag 4 f; a special's press frame is its f1. Orrubel: L1 box (44,−2,64,22), ring launch (14,−2) so both reach an 18 px enemy on the same floor; Sentry bolts 2 dmg (never ×1.5); Korvask's catch grants +15 meter once; a second Pulse Line is refused (deny).
* Hurt: 1 heart (boss attacks 1–2), 16 f hurt, 60 i-frames (the blink-off beats draw an α 0.4 silhouette outlined in `glow`), knockback (240 away, −300)·weight, 6 f hitstop. Hazards (spikes, pools, crush by a mover, Corrosant's Flood): 1 heart ignoring i-frames, 30 f freeze, respawn at the last entry spawn (a chute entry: the first footing taken after the launch; the Flood: the nearest row-16 grate) with 45 i-frames. Pogo `vy=−580`; no body contact on a pogo tick or while a special holds its victim (Brackmaw dive, Korvask press).
* L1/L2 are shoves: the victim slides at the sheet knockback but stops at the edge of the attacker's next light (its box front + the victim's half width − 8 px; L3, heavies, specials keep the full slide / launch), so a grounded L1→L2→L3 connects on a kbResist-1 target for every fighter. Refused presses are never silent: a Special / Overclock / Dash press whose 6 f buffer expires unused plays `deny` + an 18 f cue over the fighter (a special also flashes the energy bar); a Pulse Line with < 30 px of floor ahead is refused (no energy). Vellix's Blink Strike passes a boss body or stops flush at it, never ends inside. Overclock's clock stands still while the fighter is locked (intro card, ability pickup).
* Energy max 100 (125), start 50, regen 3/s (6 in Overclock); special costs 30/40/25/35/20; ultimate 40. Meter 100: hits +2/+4/+6/+5/+2/+3, bit +1, spark +10, shard +15, core +25; Overclock 720 f, dmg ×1.5, run ×1.15, dash ×1.15, reach ×1.2, kb taken ×0.5.
* Pickups: bits small 1 / medium 5 / large 20; heart +1 HP; spark +25 energy +10 meter; shard +15 meter. Drops: heart 12 % + 8 %/missing (cap 40), spark 25 % below half energy else 5 %, shard 8 % (guaranteed from rambyte/platewalker/broodcell/voltwheel). Bosses: 60/100/140 bits (large coins) + 3 sparks. Placed bits total 331; enemies 239 per full clear.
* Economy: placed bits 331; a full enemy clear 245. Enemy HP: scuttlebit 6 (9/9/12) · hopnode 9 (12) · driftmoth 6 / brinejelly 9 · glarewisp 9 (12) · spitvalve 12 · rambyte 18 (24) · platewalker 15 (21) · broodcell 21 (27), motes 3 · droplatch 9 · voltwheel 12. `kbResist`: 0 for spitvalve, broodcell, voltwheel, charging rambyte, guarding platewalker; 0.5 rambyte/platewalker otherwise; 1.0 the rest; bosses 0.
* Tallock: a fighter on a row-12 shelf gets only shelf-reaching attacks (Stack Shrug ≤ 95 px, else Slam / Disc); 3 head pogos in a row bring the **Stack Shrug** (tell 24 f, box 170 wide from 90 px over his top to 40 px under it, 1 dmg, recover 40); the Stack Slam marker follows the player in the tell, freezes at the landing x and stays through the arc; a fighter the slam lands on (or one of his hits knocks into him) is pushed clear next frame. Weftyr's Snare: a fighter coming down onto it treads it flat (snaps, no damage). Corrosant: an edge beacon at the view's top while its head is above the view; every boss exposes `focusX/focusY` for the camera. Kernauth: Root Spikes bleached bone `#f2e4c2` + ink outline + white edge, bone marks with a dashed riser, cables on a pale `#e8c79a` rim (luminance, not hue). Boss camera: frames `focusX/focusY` with the fighter (midpoint, fighter ≥ 40 px inside the sides; the view rises toward the focus only as far as the feet stay above the boss bar, screen y ≤ 444) — Corrosant's head shows from the grates, the beacon covers the floor; a boss off the view's left/right edge gets a pulsing accent chevron at that edge. A boss/enemy melee box touching the attacker's body knocks a fighter standing between body and box centre away from the body (`game.knockOrigin`).
* Boss intro card 150 f; seals drop 12 f; enrage at Tallock ≤36 / Weftyr ≤54 / Corrosant ≤72 (Flood) / Kernauth-2 ≤60 (Panic Loop); enrage tell cuts never take a telegraph below 20 f; a dead boss's shots pop and the defeat is recorded + saved at once (the module waits in the arena if uncollected); ability pickup sequence 120 f; boss death shake 12/40. Kernauth's chest core (×1) is the 150×60 band of the mass at rows 16–17 (centre 510) — reachable by every air box from a plain jump without touching the body, the rest of the mass ×0.5; the throne bursts at the phase change. Dive Bomb / Blink Slam shocks 120×30 at the base, registered on the landing frame; the Dive Bomb impacts where it arrives (a grate included, no snap to the floor) and its body deals no contact within 80 px of the lock point. Cable Sweep hurts only at its 40 px whip-tip (jump the tip, land on the slack cable). Corrosant: a hit box reaching the head is ×1.5 even over segment 0; the Wall Lunge head holds the strike height for the last 10 f of the tell; the Flood stirs 30 f, rises 20 f harmlessly, then burns 100 f (50 f of warning). Weftyr's Snare Line lies at knee height and snaps on its first catch (jump it or use the grates); its Thread Dash runs exactly along the telegraphed line (the shuttle snaps onto it as the pass begins). Tallock's Slab Swing 70×40 sweeps 90→40 px above the floor (a mid-tell jump clears it); its walk halts 16 px short of the player and its Stack Slam passes through the grates to the floor. A 0-damage hit (the Overclock shove) never rounds up on a boss.
* Room transition: freeze 6 + slide 18 + settle 4 = 28 f. Zone toast 150 f; lore card ≤240 f once per zone. Map 40 px per screen (grid in §6).
* Music: Title "Boot Sequence" 80 BPM · Driftcache "Idle Loop" 72 · Loomworks "Pressure Line" 104 · Brinevault "Facets" 96 · Nullspire "Unanswered" 84 · Rootwell "Null Pointer" 60 · Boss "Fault Handler" 150 (phase 2: 158).
